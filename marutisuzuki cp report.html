CVP Glossary

📊 Contribution

Sales revenue minus variable costs. Shows how much each unit contributes to covering fixed costs and profit.

📈 CMR (Contribution Margin Ratio)

Contribution as percentage of sales. Measures pricing power and variable cost efficiency.

⚖️ Break-even

Sales level where total contribution equals fixed costs. No profit or loss at this point.

🛡️ MOS (Margin of Safety)

Excess of actual sales over break-even sales. Shows cushion against volume declines.

⚡ DOL (Degree of Operating Leverage)

Measures how much EBIT changes for 1% change in sales. Higher DOL means more sensitivity.

🏗️ Operating Leverage

Use of fixed costs to amplify returns. Benefits volume growth but increases downside risk.
